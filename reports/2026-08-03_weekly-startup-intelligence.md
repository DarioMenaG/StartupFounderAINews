## 7. WHAT YOU SHOULD BE FOCUSING ON

### Immediate Action Items

1. **Learn Context Engineering** — This is the #1 competitive advantage for solo founders in 2026. Master RAG pipelines, MCP servers, and structured memory systems. This skill lets you build AI agents that are dramatically more reliable than competitors.

2. **Target Vertical AI** — The horizontal AI play is dominated by OpenAI/Anthropic/Google. The real opportunity is vertical AI: industry-specific solutions for healthcare, legal ($604M raised), construction, logistics, or government. Domain expertise + AI creates a moat that foundation model companies struggle to replicate.

3. **Study Defense Tech and GovTech** — $357B in U.S. GovTech spending and $18B+ flowing into defense tech. Dual-use technology (civilian + defense applications) is especially attractive to investors. This is underexplored by most indie founders.

4. **Build in Public, Validate on Reddit** — Reddit has emerged as the top free channel for startup idea validation and early traction. Communities like r/startups, r/SaaS, and r/entrepreneur are where founders are testing and iterating before spending on ads.

5. **Embrace the Solo Founder Advantage** — Solo founders are outperforming co-founder teams in 2026. Key stats:
   - 58% five-year survival rate (vs. 32% for VC-backed)
   - 10-50x higher capital efficiency
   - Co-founder conflict kills 65% of high-potential startups
   - AI tools now eliminate the need for a technical co-founder for many products

### How to Prepare Yourself Competitively

1. **Spend more time on research than building** — Skipping research is the most expensive mistake for bootstrapped founders. Deeply understand your target customer before writing code.
2. **Build your AI toolkit now** — Get fluent with Cursor, Claude, multi-agent systems, and automation platforms. These are table stakes, not advantages.
3. **Focus on regulated industries** — Healthcare, finance, defense, and government have the highest moats and the most VC interest. Regulation is your friend as a barrier to competition.
4. **Practice capital efficiency** — Bootstrapped startups survive at nearly 2x the rate of VC-backed ones. Build for revenue from day one.
5. **Network into defense/gov ecosystems** — Attend industry events, connect with defense tech VCs (Shield Capital, Razor's Edge, Decisive Point). This ecosystem is less crowded than consumer tech.
