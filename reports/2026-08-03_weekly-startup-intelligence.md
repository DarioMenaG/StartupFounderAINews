## TREND OF THE WEEK: Physical AI Goes Mainstream — Robots Enter Production

The single biggest trend this week: **AI is leaving the screen and entering the physical world at unprecedented scale.** Robotics startups have raised **$18.8 billion in 2026** so far (already surpassing 2025's full-year $15B and the 2021 peak of $14.1B). Humanoid/general-purpose robots alone account for **$8.6 billion** raised YTD — 1.8x all of 2025.

The signature deal: **Atoms** (Travis Kalanick's comeback vehicle) raised **$1.7 billion** led by a16z on July 22, merging CloudKitchens, Pronto mining automation, and transport businesses into a unified "computer for the physical world" across 110+ cities. His contrarian thesis: specialized industrial robots beat humanoids.

Meanwhile, **Walden Robotics** emerged from stealth on July 15 with **$300M** at a $1.1B valuation (Toyota, NVIDIA, Boeing backing), with robots already doing production work in a Toyota plant using Large Behavior Models. **Figure AI** enters 2026 valued at **$39 billion** with $1.9B+ total funding.

The message is clear: the era of pure-software AI is maturing. The new frontier is AI that does real things in real environments.
