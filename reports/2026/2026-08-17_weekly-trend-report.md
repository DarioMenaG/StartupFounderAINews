## 📈 Trend of the Week: "The AI Service Company"

### What It Is
A new company archetype is emerging: businesses that **sell completed work** rather than software licenses. Instead of "here's a tool to do your accounting," they say "your accounting is done." The AI does the work; the customer gets the outcome.

### Why It Matters Now
- LLMs and AI agents have crossed the capability threshold to handle complex, multi-step professional workflows
- Customers would rather buy outcomes than learn tools
- The economics work: AI service companies can deliver work at a fraction of the cost of traditional service firms while maintaining software-like margins
- YC has made this their **#1 category** for Summer 2026

### Examples Emerging
- **AI-powered tax preparation and filing** — not a tax tool, but your taxes done
- **AI insurance brokerage** — not a quoting tool, but your coverage placed
- **AI compliance reporting** — not a compliance platform, but your reports filed
- **Naïve** — not a business tools platform, but your company set up and running

### Competitive Advantage
The defensibility comes from **operational excellence**, not technology moats. Winners will be the companies that build the best process orchestration, quality assurance, and error-handling around AI agents — the "operational design" layer.
