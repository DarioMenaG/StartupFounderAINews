## WHAT YOU SHOULD FOCUS ON THIS WEEK

### 1. Pick Your Vertical — Go Deep, Not Wide
The era of horizontal SaaS is fading. Vertical AI is outpacing general software by a **3:1 ratio**. Identify a specific industry where you have domain knowledge or can build it quickly. Healthcare, legal, cybersecurity, and industrial/manufacturing are the highest-signal sectors.

### 2. Build for Outcomes, Not Features
Enterprise buyers have zero tolerance for "potential." Every pitch must answer: *"What measurable business result will this deliver in 90 days?"* Build KPI tracking into your product from day one. Track metrics like cost-per-completed-task and time-to-value.

### 3. Design for Agents, Not Humans
The next wave of commerce and enterprise software will be consumed by AI agents, not people clicking buttons. Build APIs that are machine-readable, outcome-based, and agent-native. If your product requires a human to click through a UI to get value, you're already behind.

### 4. Leverage the Solo Founder Advantage
You don't need a 10-person team to launch. Use Cursor for AI-accelerated coding, Supabase for instant backend, Vercel for deployment, and Notion as your command center. A solo founder with the right AI stack can now build and ship production software faster than a small team could 2 years ago.

### 5. Validate with Enterprise Pilots, Not Consumer Launches
Series A funding for AI startups averages **$51.9M** — 30% higher than non-AI counterparts. But investors want to see enterprise traction. Focus on landing 2-3 design partners in your target vertical before raising. Proof of outcomes > proof of concept.
