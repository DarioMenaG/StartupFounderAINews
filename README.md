# StartupFounderAINews

Weekly intelligence reports on startup trends, founder strategies, funding landscapes, and emerging opportunities — powered by AI research across TechCrunch, Reddit, Y Combinator, LinkedIn, and other trusted sources.

## What This Repo Tracks

- **Industries founders are penetrating** (AI, defense, fintech, climate, govtech, robotics, space)
- **Problems they are solving** (workflow automation, regulated industry compliance, physical-digital bridging)
- **Tools they are utilizing** (Cursor, Next.js, FastAPI, Claude, agent-driven systems)
- **Vision for the future** (AI-native services replacing entire industries, Company Brain/AI OS)
- **Your strategic playbook** (what to focus on, areas of opportunity, competitive preparation)

## Report Structure

Each weekly report contains:

1. **Macro Trend** - The single biggest signal of the week
2. **What Founders Are Building** - Industries, problems, and notable funding rounds
3. **What Tools Founders Are Using** - The modern startup stack
4. **What Investors Want** - Funding landscape and VC expectations
5. **Your Strategic Playbook** - Actionable steps and areas of opportunity
6. **Key Numbers** - Data points that matter
7. **Trend Deep Dive** - One trend explored in detail
