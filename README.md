# Startup Founder AI News

Weekly trend reports analyzing the startup ecosystem — industries, founder strategies, tools, funding patterns, and actionable insights for aspiring founders.

## Structure

```
reports/
  weekly/
    YYYY-MM-DD_weekly-startup-trend-report.md   # Weekly reports with date stamps
```

## What Each Report Covers

1. **Macro Trend of the Week** — The single most important signal founders should understand
2. **Industry Analysis** — Where capital is flowing, tiered by accessibility for new founders
3. **Tools Founders Are Using** — Current tech stacks for building, distribution, and operations
4. **Founder Vision Themes** — What the best founders are thinking about
5. **Actionable Focus Areas** — Immediate, medium-term, and strategic actions to take
6. **Key Metrics & Signals** — Data points to track week over week
7. **Contrarian Take** — A non-obvious insight to challenge conventional thinking

## Sources

Reports are researched from TechCrunch, Crunchbase, Y Combinator, Reddit (r/startups, r/SaaS, r/entrepreneur), LinkedIn, Harvard Law Forum, Fast Company, Defense News, and other trusted startup publications.

## Cadence

New reports are published weekly, every Tuesday.
