## What's Covered

Each weekly report includes:

- **Sector Analysis** -- Where capital is flowing (AI, defense, climate, fintech, biotech, govtech)
- **Major Funding Rounds** -- The biggest raises and what they signal
- **Tools & Tech Stacks** -- What founders are actually building with
- **YC Batch Insights** -- Trends from the latest Y Combinator cohort
- **VC Advice** -- What investors want to hear and what to avoid
- **Community Pulse** -- Real founder discussions from Reddit, LinkedIn, and indie hacker communities
- **Bootstrap vs. VC** -- The evolving debate and current consensus
- **Areas of Opportunity** -- Underserved markets and emerging niches
- **Action Items** -- Specific steps to prepare for your own launch

## Sources

Research is compiled from: TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, a16z, Y Combinator, Reddit (r/startups, r/SaaS, r/Entrepreneur), LinkedIn, Indie Hackers, The VC Corner, Mercury, and 50+ additional startup publications.
